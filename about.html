About CODEHUB

Your personal study material organizer

CODEHUB is a personal study portal designed to keep all your academic materials — PDFs, C++ programs, notes, and more — organized and accessible for three core subjects: DMS (Database Management System), CPP (C++ Programming), and DSU (Data Structures & Utilities).

Upload your files to each subject, filter by type, and download them anytime — all stored locally in your browser without needing any server or internet connection.

DMS

Database Management System — relational databases, SQL, normalization, transactions, indexing and queries.

CPP

C++ Programming — fundamentals, OOP, STL, file handling, templates, pointers, memory management.

DSU

Data Structures — arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, heaps, graphs and sorting algorithms.

Upload & Organize

Drag & drop files into each subject. Your materials are saved in your browser and you can download anytime.

Smart Search

Instantly search across subjects, topics and keywords to quickly navigate your study resources.

Dark / Light Mode

Toggle between dark and light themes to suit your study environment and reduce eye strain.
